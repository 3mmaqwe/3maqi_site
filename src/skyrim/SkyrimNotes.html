Azdal's Skyrim Pub2 notes

Hello these are this ones - Azdal's notes. Things I have heard from others. Enjoy my notes. I hope it may help others.

Place to work

Theres are places you can do manual labor for 100~ gold. You have to wait 12 in game hours to redo the tasks, but its a great way to get a bit of money. You can only do 1 of these jobs, so best to just do what is closer to you.

  • Solitude Sawmill - Log carrying job
  • battle-born farm - hay carrying job

Holds and cities info

Whiterun

  • very few work benches here you can use for free. Heavely gate kept. this one does not enjoy
  • prices seem to be quite steep as well.

Markarth

a free city with many free to use workshops. including - loom, ore smelter, Forge

Karthwasten

seems to be an abandoned city. has 2 silver mine, and 2 ore smelters.

Solitude

Blacksmith area is free to use, I have also built up a relationship with some blacksmiths, buys ores, and selling firewood.

if you just stand in the blacksmith area people will do business with you.
