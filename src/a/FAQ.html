Hi here are some common issues ive gotten from people emailing me. I figured I put down issues people have so people can check here and see if they have a similar issue. I also just have other questions that maybe people would ask me.

Image can't be submitted to imgur / uploaded to google

Mostly commonly it is probably for this reason:

if you have a free account from neocites made after 2024 you have to use iframes as a workaround for drawbox to work.(aka hosting the page elsewhere) you could host your drawbox on github pages or nekoweb, or another site that has Non-strict Content Security Policy for free (use external scripts). an example of the iframe mirroring can be seen at my second neocities page: https://drawbox.neocities.org/ alternatively you could swtich to a paid account for neocities. thats what I do.

it could also be that your testing in localhost or offline. Image uploading only works on a live server ive noticed.

Failed to load images.

first make sure that you have the sheet set to general access - so that "Anyone on the internet with the link can view". Also make sure that you have 5 columns on your google sheet. also again make sure you are testing in a local host enviroment, just opening the html on your browser will cause drawbox to not work properly.
