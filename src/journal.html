Reflections, Ramblings and Reading - 01/20/2026

So I guess ive been reflecting on my site as of late. I feel like i have learned, and seen a lot more of what my site can look and feel like. At least to when I first started. I also have gotten a lot more foot traffic then I thought I would when I entered a contest for my site. Which is cool of course don't get me wrong! I just never really expected to even get more then 300 views or so to be quite honest. I dont think I even really planned to spend this much time on my site either. Plus it being the new year, its always creates a space in which your reflective I suppose. I always find myself reflecting after the year ends more. December can be quite a busy month, So January leads me to just have more time to myself as well.

I never really made any new years resolutions. However, I keep finding new ones to do. For one, I have started reading again. I used to read all the time in middle school, and it was the one thing I really prided myself on. I never suffered from "gifted kid" burnout growing up - math was really hard for me, I had trouble spelling even basic words, my writing was difficult to read as well, and I never learned my times tables. I think reading was really the only thing I didnt stuggle with. I think art and reading were really my only two things I felt a bit prideful of. When i entered high school though I kinda stopped reading for fun. Maybe the rise social media and the smart phone may have been partly at play. But I don't really like have a concerte memory of me like giving up reading persay. Its just a hobby of mine that I kinda lost. So It's really been a joy picking up and reading again. Its not really even that I started reading in 2026 - I have a earlier journal entry talking about it if you scroll down bit in 2025. But i guess now since its 2026 I have made the offical new years resolution to read! I just finished the book "Howl's Moving Castle", and I really enjoyed it!

Kinokuniya

When my cousins were visiting me and my family, we went to Mitsuwa Marketplace in the suburbs - its a really big grocery store, but it also has a food court, and other small vendors. Inside Mitsuwa is a book store called Kinokuniya. Its a bookstore that has anime, books, stationary - from japan. My cousins are really into anime so they really wanted to go check it out. Ive been to Mitsuwa many times, and now that I live in chicago, its not really as novel to me as it used to be - but I thought it would be cool to check out the book store. Plus my mom wanted to make ramen for the family, so I needed to go to get ingredents for that.

At the store I bought a few things. An Art book by the illustrator Mateusz Urbanowicz titled: Toyko Storefronts - The Artworks of Mateusz Urbanowicz which I didnt realize I bought it at the only us store that sells it :o! If you (the reader) didnt already know, I do a lot of watercolor paintings, so i thought it would be a good idea to get a nice art book with watercolor illustations as inspiration and also guidance for my own practice. I really like art books as well, its something I want to build a large collection of. I think it would be cool to have enough to have a mini library one day. I was always frustrated when i would go to my local library and there were like no art books at all! An additional bonus thing as well is just discovering this artist. I feel like its only social media where I find new artists, so its just cool to find an amazing watercolor artist in a bookstore of all places! His site that I linked before is also a good resource as well, I will def being checking that out more as well.
I also got Frieren Beyond the Journey's End- Book 1 & 2 - story by Kanehito Yamada and art by Tsukasa Abe. There were more books then that, but I didnt want to get all 9 books before making sure I liked reading the first 2. I also don't want to spoil the most recent season to myself haha (also the books are 11.99 each ;-;). Season 2 just came out!

The last book I got was Howl's Moving Castle by Diana Wynee Jones. One of my favorite movies is Howl's Moving Castle, and I thought it would be fun to read the orignal book! reading the book, it was kinda funny to find out how many creative changes the movie had made. For one, in the movie, Sophie's sisters are just a footnote - barely having any plot relvance at all. where in the book, both the sisters are much more fleshed out and actively contribute to the plot. Also, some how howl is even more meledramatic and moody then in the movie omg. its so funny. Sophie is also really sassy and bossy in the best way. The story its self is also just very different. I still really like the movie, dont get me wrong, but I actually think i like the book even more to be honest!

I might honestly re-read the book again, and the movie and write a more fleshed out post comparing the the two.

Diana Wynne Jones has also written over 40 books, so I do want to check out other stories that she has written. I also just found out there are 2 additional books in howl's moving castle, so that is on my reading list. Maybe I need to make book list. I already have a movie list on my phone. I think it might be cute to move over my watch list to my site at some point. Maybe with a table, so I can like add my thoughts about the movie too.

Coffea - 12/26/2025

I find myself yet another day in which this evening there is nothing to do but to figure out how to entertain myself. Normally I would also have the labor of figuring out what to eat for dinner as well, but past me was kind enough to save leftovers for me, and buy veggies for me to boil - thanks past me. While I was eating my leftovers this evening I had remembered a conversation I had with one of my coworkers over lunch. I had told him how disconnected I feel from the things we consume daily. One thing I had realized was that I have no idea how coffee is grown. I didnt even know what kinda of plant it came from. What it from a tree, a bush, vines? I actually had no image in my mind on how they grew, on how it looked. But yet Ive had coffee, and so many people in the US consume it daily. Coffee was a mystery to me. Many things that I eat everyday, are a mystery to me.

To answer my own question (and to find something to entertain me that is not mindless doomscrolling) I looked it up. What is a coffee plant, how does it grow? I realized that i dont even know the name of the plant that produces coffee. Maybe this is very silly to thoses aware of the whole process of making coffee. Its not that im unaware of ANY plants and how they grow. I used to live by corn and soy fields, have gone to my share fare of orchards and have observed my mom grow varies plants in her backyard. I just had this fixation with coffee in particular because for one, you can only ever by it preroasted. So I do not even know what a bean looked like preroasted. From where that bean came from? no fuckin idea. Plus, coffee is not grown in the us (outside of hawaii), so its not like I could just like stumble upon a farm that grows it.

Coffea

No, that is not a mispelling of Coffee. The name of the tree that grows coffee cherries (and the beans inside the cherry) is called Coffea. I was like so starstuck when I saw a picture of that the tree branch look liked. The fruit that it comes from has such a lovely red color, and the way the berries sit and grow on the tree so bunched together, and the leaves shape!

i also found a cool youtube video called Make a Cup of Coffee Starting From Scratch | Coffea arabica that showed from harvest to cup of coffee, the process of making coffee. I think the coolest part of the video was seeing how pale white the beans are before roasting it.

I think learning about the Coffea tree, and seeing the process of how it was made, helped me feel a bit less disconnected to world around me. I feel like there are so many things that I just absolutely know very little about. I feel a great amount of disconnect to the things I interact with, the things i eat, the places i go to. So, maybe I should try to understand them a bit better to ground myself. Maybe its the byproduct of living in such a industrialized place. Not to say we should abandon the industrialize world. I very much enjoy the internet and all the other luxuries I am lucky to have.

I do still feel disconnected. another one of my many confusing feelings. I seem to have alot of thoses. I do feel better then when I wrote my last journal, however so thats good. I do find myself just zoning into my phone alot too, and I could see that contributing to the feeling of disconnection.

The Stereo Realist

I also went to a local second hand shop in my home town. its one of those places that kinda hand select items to sell, so a bit more thought into what comes in then for example, goodwill or savors. I saw there was a retro camera, retro lightmeter, and camera filters for sale. The prices seems actually really cheap so I decided to buy it. I later found out the lens i bought and the camera are NOT compatable at all... lol. Also the way the camera works is.. interesting as well. most cameras just take one photo at a time, on one film frame at a time. This camera that i brought though is meant to take 3d images.

According to the wikipedia entry for this camera, The Stereo Realist, takes 2 photos at a time, for the purpose of creating a 3d image.

heres more info about stereo cameras from wikipedia:

"A stereo camera is a type of camera with two or more lenses with a separate image sensor or film frame for each lens. This allows the camera to simulate human binocular vision, and therefore gives it the ability to capture three-dimensional images, a process known as stereo photography"

pretty crazy huh. I actually dont really know how to operate it at all. I have used retro cameras in the past, but its been years since ive done it. I plan to go to a camera store later today and hopefully someone at the shop can help me load the film and figure out how to take photos.... Otherwiser i suppose youtube would work too. but having someone show you hands on is very helpful :). Plus I think they also develop film too so that would be cool resource to learn more about.

Rolleinar Lens Filter Set

the lens case I bought is a small leather case of different filters used for old camera called the Rolleiflex. Its kinda the bigger and insane find. I wont disclose how much I paid for it just because i dont really want anyone to know until I get it checked out. I see sets like this that are retailing for almost 1k...
