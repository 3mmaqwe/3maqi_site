Photography

Back in september I had bought a camera. I have a short journal entry where I briefly talk about it. But, pretty much i just have an old camera that takes 2 photos at once called the Stereo Realist. I found it one day at a thrift shop in my hometown, and it actually still works. I had to grease it slightly to unstick the film roll progressor. But i was actually able to put in film into it, and take photos with the camera. I think the coolest thing about this camera is that it contains no electronic parts what so ever.

I actually did learn how to take photos in college - learning things like stutter speed, iso, f-stop, lightmetering and also measuring distance to make sure what your filming is in focus (i was briefly a film major). I actually really enjoy the process of planning what to shoot, light metering, ajusting my camera settings and then finally taking the photo!
