My First Phaser Platform

How to Play

The Goal is to collect as many stars as you can. Every time you clear the game area of stars, new ones appear, but a bomb also spawns. hit the bomb and its game over!

i also added a game over effect, which turns the screen red at the end

Controls:

  • Use the arrow keys to move left or right
  • The up arrow key makes you jump
